Space & layout

A spacing scale, corner radii, and the touch-target minimums that are not open to negotiation.

Spacing

Use layout gap, not per-element margins. Margins collapse and double unpredictably.

Touch targets

Non-negotiable

WCAG 2.5.5 / 2.5.8. Users include people with motor impairment and older people on small, cheap screens. This is a floor, not a target.

TokenValueWhen
min44pxMINIMUM interactive target. Non-negotiable — WCAG 2.5.5 / 2.5.8.

Surfaces

Cards lift off the bone ground as white surfaces. Hierarchy comes from the surface change, not from shadows a cheap phone has to paint.

Prefer a left status rail to icon circles in lists: faster to scan, no cross-cultural icon ambiguity, and far cheaper to render in a long list.

A known limitation

Card borders are #E4DFD4 — 1.33:1 on white. WCAG §1.4.11 covers controls and meaningful state, not decorative grouping, so this conforms. But it still reads poorly in bright outdoor light, which is the real usage condition. Let the status rails carry structure where it matters.
